JuliaBayes is a shared home for Bayesian software in Julia, making it easier to collaborate, share maintenance, and keep packages supported over time.

We’re open to collaborate with everyone, across different probabilistic programming languages, research groups, and institutions. Most of us contribute alongside our day jobs, so contributions of all sizes are welcome.

Want to bring your package into the org, or just want to discuss with us? Join us in #bayes on Julia Slack to chat!
