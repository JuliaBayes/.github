# Code of conduct

JuliaBayes welcomes people across backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and institutions.  
This applies in our repositories, events, chat, and related conversations.

JuliaBayes is part of the wider Julia community. These guidelines supplement the [Julia community standards](https://julialang.org/community/standards/).

## Working together

- Treat people with respect.
- Explain disagreements with reasons and evidence.
- Personal attacks will not be tolerated.
- Respect boundaries, accessibility needs, names, pronouns, and requests to stop contact.
- Give people room to learn and ask questions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, sharing private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.  
The same rules apply to all members of the community, and all persons who interact with the community insofar as possible.

## If something goes wrong

Message an uninvolved Steering Committee member privately on [Julia Slack](https://julialang.org/slack/) or contact the [Julia Stewards](https://julialang.org/community/stewards/) directly.  
Anyone named in a complaint or otherwise conflicted must not handle it or decide its outcome.  
Your contact helps find an independent handler if needed, without requiring you to approach the person concerned.

The people handling it acknowledge your message, explain next steps, and give those involved a fair chance to respond where appropriate.  
They may issue a warning, remove content, limit participation, or exclude someone.

Reports and records stay with people who need them to handle the concern and have no conflict of interest.  
Decisions are final and not subject to appeal. In some circumstances reasons for decisions may not be given, however we endeavour to give a reason when possible and appropriate.
